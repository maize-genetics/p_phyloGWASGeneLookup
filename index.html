Poaceae Gene Lookup

Climate association, selection, and expression evidence from p_phyloGWAS.

Accepts maize v5 (Zm00001eb…), rice IRGSP-1.0 (Os…t…), sorghum v3 / Phytozome 454 (Sobic.…), and wheat v2 / IWGSC RefSeq v2.1 (TraesCS…) gene IDs, with or without a transcript suffix. Sorghum v5 gene IDs are not yet supported — see the README.

Data snapshot built from p_phyloGWAS stages 08 (climate association), 09 (RELAX molecular evolution), and 10 (differential expression). See this repo's README for methodology and caveats.
